## How the sweep was run

`run_triggers.py` stops at the first session that is not `ok`, which is right for a quota
error and stopped the first sweep at the row 14 timeout. Rows 15-60 and the re-run were
driven by a small loop over the same `evaluate()` function that continues past a timeout
and stops on a usage limit or process error.
